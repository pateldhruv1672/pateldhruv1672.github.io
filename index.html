About

I research embodied intelligence and engineer systems that can be trusted to run.

I’m Dhruv Patel, a graduate researcher in SJSU’s Robotics & Digital Twin Lab. My work asks how language-conditioned agents can perceive a real environment, ground intent in persistent spatial memory, and execute through bounded robotics tools rather than uncontrolled model outputs.

Before graduate school, I built production AI and distributed systems at Bain & Company and Samsung Research. I bring those reliability habits into research: explicit ownership, typed interfaces, observability, reproducible experiments, safety gates, and documented failure modes.
